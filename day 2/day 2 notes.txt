#Day 2: INTEREFACES AND CABLES
- Copper cables(UTP/STP):shielded and unshielded twisted pairs. Most common for LANs. uses electrical signals.
- Fiber Optic cables: uses light to transmit data. used for long distances and high bandwidth. Immune to electro magnetic 		      interference(EMI)
- RJ45 connectors: standard connector for ethernet copper cables.
- Ethernet standards: outlines the speeds and distances for different categories of cables(e.g.,CAT5e,CAT6)
