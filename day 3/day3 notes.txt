DAY 3: TCP/IP & OSI models
	PROTOCOLS: set of rules that determine how data should be communicated between devices.
	STANDARD: agreed specifications upon which protocols should work.
	ARPA: Advanced Research Project Agency. They created the ARPANET.
	IEEE: Institute of Electrical and Electronic Engineering.
		this body also define standards.
		they design some of the standards such as
		Ethernet(802.3)
		Wi-fi(802.11)
	IETF: Internet Engineering Task Force.
	TCP/IP MODEL: TCP stands for Transmission Control Protocol. under this we have the following layers:
			Application, Transport, Network/Internet, Datalink, Physical.
	ISO: International Organization of Standardization.
	OSI MODEL: OSI stands for Open Systems Interconnection. Under this we have the following layers:
			Application, Presentation, Session, Transport, Network, Datalink, Physical.

	transfer of information between this layers happen by encapsulation and decapsulation.
